Privacy Policy — Slice Rush

Last updated: 2026-09-24

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Slice Rush ("the app") handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

The app does not create an account and does not collect or send personal information such as your name or email address to any server of ours.

  • Game progress (level reached, stars per level, best combo, coins, which blade you own and have equipped, and your daily-reward streak) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage (localStorage). It never leaves your device and is lost if you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. There is one ad format only: an optional rewarded video — you can watch one to carry on after hitting a bar, or to double the coins from a finished level. Watching is entirely optional; if you skip it the game continues normally. There are no banners and no ads between levels.

AdMob may collect certain device data in order to serve and measure ads (for example advertising ID, device type, approximate location/IP, interaction data). This is governed by Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit ad personalisation from your device settings (Google → Ads).

3. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. Blades are bought with coins earned by playing, and coins cannot be bought with money.

4. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. AdMob settings are used to keep ad content appropriate.

5. Internet connection

The game itself runs offline; the internet is used only to load the rewarded video. With no connection the game plays exactly the same, and the rewarded video simply is not offered.

6. Data security

Because progress data is kept only on your device, it is never transmitted or shared by us.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
