Privacy Policy — Fruit Hole

Last updated: 2026-08-14

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Fruit Hole ("the app") handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

The app does not create an account and does not collect or send personal information such as your name or email address to any server of ours.

  • Game progress (level, stars, collected fruit, upgrades, boosters, daily missions, achievements and your sound preference) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage (localStorage). It never leaves your device and is lost if you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an interstitial between levels, a banner during play, and an optional rewarded video when your time runs out. Watching the rewarded video is entirely optional; if you skip it the game continues normally.

AdMob may collect certain device data in order to serve and measure ads (for example advertising ID, device type, approximate location/IP, interaction data). This is governed by Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit ad personalisation from your device settings (Google → Ads).

3. In-app purchases

The app offers optional purchases (ad-free version, fruit packs, starter pack). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; your card details and payment information are never sent to us and are never stored by us. Which products you own is recorded only on your device.

4. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. AdMob settings are used to keep ad content appropriate.

5. Internet connection

The game itself runs offline; the internet is used only to load ads. With no connection the game plays exactly the same, without ads.

6. Data security

Because progress data is kept only on your device, it is never transmitted or shared by us.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
